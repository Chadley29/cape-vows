Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Cape Vows is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and all applicable South African data protection laws. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

1. Who We Are

Cape Vows is an online wedding directory dedicated to the Western Cape, South Africa. We operate the website at capevows.co.za.

For the purposes of POPIA, Cape Vows is the Responsible Party — the entity that determines the purpose and means of processing your personal information.

Contact: hello@capevows.co.za

2. What Personal Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is necessary for the purposes described in this policy. This may include:

Information you provide directly

  • Your name and email address when you contact us via email
  • Enquiry details or messages you send to hello@capevows.co.za
  • Business details if you enquire about listing your venue or services

Information collected automatically

  • Website usage data via Google Analytics (pages visited, time on site, device type, approximate location by country/city)
  • IP address and browser type (collected automatically by web servers and analytics tools)

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect payment information — Cape Vows currently has no e-commerce functionality
  • We do not collect sensitive personal information such as identity numbers, race, health data, or financial records
  • We do not run newsletter sign-ups or mailing lists at this time

3. Why We Collect Your Information (Purpose)

Under POPIA, we may only process personal information for specific, explicitly defined, and lawful purposes. We collect and use your information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries — when you email us, we use your name and email address to reply
  • To manage venue and vendor listings — if you are a venue or vendor owner, we may use your business contact details to maintain accurate directory listings
  • To improve our website — anonymised analytics data helps us understand how visitors use Cape Vows and make it better
  • To comply with legal obligations — we may process information if required to do so by law

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information on the following lawful grounds under POPIA:

  • Consent — where you have voluntarily provided your information (e.g. by emailing us)
  • Legitimate interest — where processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided these do not override your rights (e.g. website analytics, maintaining accurate listings)
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to process information by applicable law

5. How We Store and Protect Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against loss, damage, unauthorised access, or disclosure. Specifically:

  • Our website is served over HTTPS (SSL encryption)
  • Email correspondence is stored within Google Workspace, which applies industry-standard security measures
  • We do not store personal information in unsecured spreadsheets or unencrypted local files
  • Access to any personal information we hold is limited to Cape Vows personnel only

Please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — we use Google Analytics to analyse website traffic. Google processes anonymised usage data on our behalf. You can read Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or regulatory authority, we may disclose personal information
  • Business transfer — in the unlikely event Cape Vows is sold or transferred, personal information held may form part of the transferred assets, subject to the same privacy protections

7. Retention of Personal Information

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law.

  • Email correspondence — retained for up to 2 years, then deleted unless an ongoing relationship requires longer retention
  • Venue and vendor listing information — retained for as long as the listing is active. Venues and vendors may request removal at any time
  • Analytics data — Google Analytics retains data in accordance with our configured retention settings (currently 14 months)

8. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject under POPIA, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access — you may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to correction — you may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information
  • Right to deletion — you may request that we delete your personal information, subject to any legal obligations we may have to retain it
  • Right to object — you may object to the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time
  • Right to lodge a complaint — you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa (see Section 10 below)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@capevows.co.za. We will respond within a reasonable time and no later than 30 days.

9. Cookies and Analytics

Cape Vows uses Google Analytics to collect anonymised information about how visitors use our website. Google Analytics uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to collect this data.

The information collected includes pages visited, time spent on the site, device and browser type, and approximate geographic location (country/city level). This information is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings.

Cape Vows does not use any other tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party retargeting tools.

10. The Information Regulator

The Information Regulator is the South African authority responsible for overseeing compliance with POPIA. If you believe your rights under POPIA have been violated and we have not adequately addressed your concern, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator:

  • Website: www.inforegulator.org.za
  • Email: inforeg@justice.gov.za
  • Address: JD House, 27 Stiemens Street, Braamfontein, Johannesburg, 2001

11. Third-Party Websites

Cape Vows contains links to venue and vendor websites. These third-party websites have their own privacy policies and we have no control over their content or practices. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

12. Children's Privacy

Cape Vows is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. We will indicate the date of the most recent update at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
